About Me

I am an AI research engineer based in Paris with a background in electronic engineering and a Master's degree in Artificial Intelligence from Université Paris-Saclay. My current research interests are resource-efficient machine learning, robot learning, world models, multimodal learning, and agentic AI.

My recent work spans deep learning for under-resourced speech synthesis, agent-based systems for structured-data analysis, and mobile robotics. I am particularly interested in how increasingly capable learning-based models can be made practical for deployment on resource-constrained robotic platforms.

Research Vision

My long-term research goal is to develop learning-based systems that can operate effectively on real robotic platforms. I am particularly interested in how model design, adaptive computation, and efficient representations can preserve task performance while reducing the memory, latency, and energy requirements of deployed AI systems.

Selected Projects

Low-Resource Walloon Speech Synthesis

LISN, Université Paris-Saclay · 2024

Developed and evaluated VITS-based text-to-speech systems for Walloon using recordings from two speakers. The study compared grapheme and phoneme input representations and Walloon-only training with transfer learning from French-pretrained models. Evaluation combined objective speech metrics with a perceptual study involving 23 native Walloon speakers.

Paper / Code and models

Agent-Based Natural-Language Interface for Structured Data

Capital Fund Management · 2025

Developed an analyst-facing system that translated natural-language questions into executable SQL for internal financial-data analysis. The agent determined which information and tools were needed before SQL generation, narrowing the database context through schema retrieval and table relationships instead of passing the full database description to the model.

The system reached 87.5% end-to-end accuracy on a 48-question internal benchmark, while hybrid table retrieval reached 95% top-k accuracy.

Semi-Autonomous Rover for Precision Agriculture

Mobile Robotics Laboratory, University of São Paulo · 2014

Contributed to a semi-autonomous agricultural rover designed to support soil-composition measurements using laser-induced breakdown spectroscopy (LIBS). I developed and integrated the rover's propulsion and steering control system, selected electronic components under power and environmental constraints, and participated in prototype and controlled field testing.
